## How can you plan ahead so tax season holds no surprises?

We look ahead so you can plan with confidence. In practice, that means a conversation before the move, not a bill after it.

- **Entity and ownership questions:** Is your current structure still the right one as the business grows or partners change?
- **Estimated payments:** Set from where you actually stand this year, not a guess from last year.
- **Timing a purchase or sale:** We look at the tax effect before you sign, when there's still room to adjust.
- **Succession planning:** Handing off or selling the business takes years of groundwork, and the tax side shapes the outcome.
