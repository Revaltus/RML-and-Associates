## Your practice runs on your expertise, not your paperwork

You are great with clients. The financial side is what eats your evenings. Income swings from month to month, estimated tax payments are hard to predict, and deadlines pile up while you are with a patient, a client or a case. RML and Associates CPAs is the CPA firm Laramie, WY professionals call when they want that side of the practice handled by people who answer the phone. We are locally owned, so the person who knows your practice is the person who picks up. You keep doing the work you trained for. We keep the numbers and the calendar in order.

## Business tax work built around how your practice earns

A larger-than-expected tax bill is usually what sends a practice owner looking for a new CPA. It rarely comes from nowhere. It comes from nobody looking at the numbers until April.

We prepare and file your [business tax](/business-tax) returns at the state and federal level, and we handle local and state sales tax compliance when your practice collects it. We also produce profit and loss statements, so you can see how the practice is actually doing during the year, not after it closes.

The work does not stop when the return is filed. We plan through the year and keep track of your deadlines, so filings and payments never sneak up on you. When something changes, such as a new hire, a new location or a big purchase, call us before it happens. A short conversation now is easier than a surprise later.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: personal-return-planning.jpg | alt: "Accountant and professional discussing personal income tax return paperwork at a table" | query: "accountant explaining tax return to client" -->
## Personal returns that account for your practice income

You are not just a business. Your practice income lands on your personal return, and the two need to agree.

We prepare your [personal income tax](/personal-income-tax) return alongside the business return, so one team sees both sets of numbers. That means fewer gaps, fewer questions in March and no last-minute scramble to find a figure that lives in someone else's file.

You get accurate, on-time returns, filed electronically with direct deposit for any refund. If you need more time, we file the extension. We also calculate your estimated tax payments, so you are paying based on what the practice is earning now, not a guess from last year. Income that swings by month is exactly why those estimates matter.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: practice-financial-planning.jpg | alt: "Two accountants and a practice owner reviewing financial reports on a laptop in an office" | query: "accountants reviewing financial reports with client" -->
## Accounting and advisory that help you plan ahead

We look ahead so you can plan with confidence. For a practice owner, that means knowing whether you can hire another associate, replace the equipment or open a second location before you commit, not after.

That starts with clean, timely [accounting](/accounting). When your financials are current, [advisory](/advisory) conversations get specific. Megan Hanneman, CGMA, works with owners on budgeting, forecasting and the handful of numbers that show how the practice is doing.

A practical example: many practices still run desktop accounting software. Liz Powers, CPA, helps owners move from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online, so reports are available when you need them and not only when someone is in the office.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Professional services

**Q: Do you work with professional practices outside Laramie?**
A: Yes. We are based in Laramie and serve professional services firms and practices across Wyoming, Nebraska and Colorado. Because the firm is locally owned, the person who knows your practice is the person who answers your call. Start with a free consultation to see whether we are the right fit.

**Q: Why are estimated taxes so hard for practice owners?**
A: When income arrives unevenly, last year's number is a poor guide to this year's payments. We calculate your estimated taxes from your current figures and coordinate the business and personal returns, so each payment reflects what the practice is actually earning. That avoids both underpaying and tying up cash you need.

**Q: Can you prepare my business and personal returns together?**
A: Yes, and we recommend it. When one team sees both the practice's profit and your personal return, income lines up and nothing falls between two preparers. You get on-time returns filed electronically, extensions when you need them, and estimated payments that match both sets of numbers.

**Q: Can you help us move from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online?**
A: Yes. Liz Powers, CPA, helps practices move from desktop accounting software such as QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online. The benefit is practical: current reports available whenever you need them, and cleaner financials to base decisions about hiring, equipment or expansion on.

**Q: What should I bring to the free consultation?**
A: Bring last year's tax return if you have it, plus a rough picture of how your practice earns: who pays you, how often and about how much. You do not need organized files. We will listen, explain where we can help and answer your questions.
