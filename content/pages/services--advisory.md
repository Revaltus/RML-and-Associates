## Year-round business tax strategy, not just an April filing

Most owners hear from their CPA twice a year: once to collect documents, once to deliver a bill. By then, every decision that shaped the result has already been made.

Business advisory services in Laramie, WY should work the other way around. Our [Business Tax](/business-tax) service pairs state and federal income tax preparation and sales tax compliance with year-round planning and deadline management. You see the tax result before it happens.

Three decision points come up again and again:

- Timing a major purchase, such as equipment or a vehicle, so it lands in the year it helps most.
- Estimating quarterly liability from your real numbers, not last year's.
- Reviewing the profit and loss statement mid-year, while there is still time to act on it.

Megan Hanneman, CGMA, brings budgeting and forecasting experience to these conversations for owners and management teams who want numbers they can steer by. The goal is confidence, not just compliance.

## Advisory for farms and ranches, from drought deferrals to succession

A dry summer in Wyoming, Nebraska or Colorado can force a herd sale earlier than you planned. That sale lands on your tax return whether the year was good or not. Federal rules may allow some of that income to be deferred, but whether they apply depends on your herd, your location and your timing. The time to ask is before you sell.

Succession is the other big question. At some point the operation passes to the next generation, and the tax cost of how it passes can be large. A CPA can help you map it out well ahead of time:

- Who takes over management, and who simply owns a share.
- How land, equipment and livestock move between generations.
- How to treat heirs who are not involved in the day-to-day work.

The answers are practical, and they are easier to reach when no one is in a hurry. Our [agriculture](/industries/agriculture) clients get that unhurried conversation. The first one is free.

## Real estate tax advisory before you close the deal

The expensive surprises in real estate happen after the closing table. A flip finishes, a big commission clears, and the tax bill arrives with no plan behind it.

For agents and brokers, the trouble is irregular 1099 income. Commissions come in lumps, so a flat quarterly estimate rarely fits. Mileage, marketing and other business expenses also need to be tracked to be counted.

For portfolio investors, the trigger is usually growth: a second or third property, or a portfolio that has outgrown how it is currently set up.

That is why our real estate tax advisory in Laramie, WY starts before the deal. We model the tax impact of buying, flipping, holding or exchanging a property before you commit. You compare the outcomes while you can still change your mind, instead of finding out afterward. See more on our [real estate](/industries/real-estate) page.

## Restaurant owners: make sure you are capturing your tip credits

Restaurants run on thin margins and high volume. When the margin is that tight, a missed credit hurts.

We advise restaurant owners on tip credits and how they affect the business's tax position. We also cover the pieces around them: sales tax compliance and a regular look at the profit and loss statement, so you know which nights, menus and costs are making money. Credit rules and amounts change, so we confirm the current details against your actual records before you count on anything.

If you have never had a CPA check whether your credits are being captured, that is a good first question for a free consultation. Details are on our [restaurants](/industries/restaurants) page.

Our non-profit financial advisory in Laramie, WY is built for the moments when pressure spikes: a funder requires an audit, a filing was missed, the board turns over, or a bookkeeper or finance staff member leaves. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Advisory services

**Q: What does CPA advisory include at RML and Associates?**
A: Advisory means we work with you before decisions are made. That includes year-round business tax strategy, mid-year profit and loss reviews, and questions about buying, selling or passing on an operation. RML and Associates CPAs advises farms and ranches, real estate investors, restaurants and non-profits across Wyoming, Nebraska and Colorado.

**Q: Do you offer a free consultation?**
A: Yes. The first conversation is free. You describe your operation, we look at upcoming deadlines or decisions, and you leave with a clear next step. Whether you run a ranch, a restaurant, a real estate portfolio or a non-profit, use the contact page and expect a prompt reply.

**Q: Can you help with drought deferrals on a ranch?**
A: Drought can force livestock sales earlier than planned, and federal tax rules may allow some of that income to be deferred. Whether it applies depends on your herd, your location and your timing. Call before you sell, and we will review the facts with you rather than guess.

**Q: How can a CPA help real estate agents with estimated taxes?**
A: Commission income arrives unevenly, so a flat quarterly payment often misses. We estimate liability from your actual closings and expenses, such as mileage and marketing, and adjust through the year. For investors, we model the tax impact of buying, flipping, holding or exchanging a property before you commit.

**Q: Can you help a non-profit with Form 990 and state compliance?**
A: Yes. We prepare Form 990 and 990-T and keep you compliant with state law, which protects your tax-exempt status. If a funder requires an audit, a filing was missed or a finance staff member leaves, call early. Clear financials for your board and funders are part of the work.
