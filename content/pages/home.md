You run the operation. Here is what owners in each line of work bring us.

### Agriculture and ranching
icon: Home

Weather decides half your year. When drought forces you to sell livestock, we work out whether deferring that income makes sense. We also sit down early on succession planning, so the next generation inherits an operation and not a tax problem. [See how we work with farms and ranches](/industries/agriculture)

### Restaurants
icon: Receipt

Tip credits are worth real money and easy to get wrong. We make sure you claim the credit you've earned and keep the records behind it, so thin margins don't get thinner. [See how we work with restaurants](/industries/restaurants)

### Retail
icon: CreditCard

Retail owners bring us two questions: is sales tax filed correctly at the state and local level, and what are we really earning on what we sell? We answer both in plain numbers. [See how we work with retailers](/industries/retail)

### Professional services
icon: Briefcase

Practice owners ask how to pay themselves, when estimated payments are due, and whether the practice is as profitable as it feels. You get a clear answer on each. [See how we work with professional practices](/industries/professional-services)

### Real estate
icon: Building

Agents and brokers live on irregular commission-based 1099 income, which makes estimated taxes hard to guess. Portfolio investors get the surprise bill after a big sale or flip. We model the tax impact of buying, flipping, holding or exchanging a property before you close. [See how we work with real estate clients](/industries/real-estate)

### Nonprofits
icon: ShieldCheck

Looking for a CPA for non-profit organizations near me? Form 990 and 990-T filings are complex, and a late or wrong filing can put your tax-exempt status at risk. We step in when a funder requires an audit or a finance staff member leaves. Compliance without the stress. [See how we work with nonprofits](/industries/nonprofits)
