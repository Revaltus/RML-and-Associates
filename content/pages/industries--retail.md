## Accounting that keeps pace with your sales floor

If you run a boutique, a specialty shop or a downtown storefront in Laramie, your week belongs to customers and shelves. The numbers wait until Sunday night, then until the next Sunday night. RML and Associates CPAs is a locally owned CPA firm, and we shape our work around how your store actually runs, not around a template.

Retail accounting services here come down to three things: sales tax filed correctly, books that tie to your point of sale, and a business tax return with no unpleasant surprises. Looking for a CPA for your retail store in Laramie? Call us. We'll walk you through where you stand and make sure it makes sense.

## Sales tax compliance across Wyoming, Nebraska and Colorado

Collecting the right tax is one job. Remitting it on time, to the right state, is another. Wyoming, Nebraska and Colorado each run their own sales tax system with their own forms and due dates. That matters the moment you ship an order across the state line or a customer from Fort Collins or Cheyenne buys at your register.

Sales tax compliance is part of our [Business Tax work](/business-tax), so it sits alongside your returns instead of being handled by someone who has never seen your books. Here is what we keep an eye on for you:

- Filing deadlines in each state where you're registered
- Whether the tax you collect matches the sales you reported
- Whether selling to customers in another state creates a filing obligation

We check each state's current rules before we advise you, because they change. If you've ever wondered whether you're collecting too little, too much, or in the wrong place, that's a good first question to bring us.

## Point of sale reports that match your books

Here's a common story. The point of sale report says one number, the bank says another, and you're not sure which to believe. Usually nothing is wrong. Card processors deposit net of fees, refunds post on different days, and discounts and gift cards don't land where you'd expect.

We reconcile daily sales, card processor deposits, refunds and discounts so your books tie out to both the register and the bank. That's the heart of point of sale bookkeeping in Laramie: finding the reason for every difference, then fixing the cause so next month starts clean. We also track cost of goods sold carefully, because it sets your true margins and flows straight into your tax return.

If you're a small retail business accountant's ideal client, you're probably someone who wants reports you can trust and a quick answer when you call. You'll get both.

## Business tax returns without the holiday-season surprise

A strong fourth quarter feels great until March. Then comes the tax bill nobody budgeted for, especially after a big purchase of fixtures, a build-out or new equipment.

We prepare your state and federal income tax returns as part of our [Business Tax services](/business-tax), and we don't wait for filing season to start the conversation. Throughout the year we monitor deadlines, calculate estimated taxes and check your year-to-date results against what you owe. You learn about a large bill in September, while there's still time to make choices about it, not in the spring.

That's what looking ahead means for a retailer. You decide on that new display case, a second register or extra seasonal stock knowing what it does to your tax picture.

## Profit and loss statements you can use to make decisions

Should you add a part-timer for the holidays? Renew the lease? Stay open later on Fridays? Open a second location? Those are real decisions, and a regular profit and loss statement answers them with your own numbers instead of a hunch.

We prepare profit and loss statements that show sales, cost of goods sold, payroll costs as a share of revenue, rent and what's left. When a number moves, we tell you what we see and what it might mean. Megan Hanneman, CGMA, brings management-accounting experience in budgeting, forecasting and performance tracking, so you can compare where the store is with where you want it to be. For deeper questions, see our [advisory services](/advisory).

We're also an independent member of the BDO Alliance USA, which gives a local firm access to a wider network of resources.

Looking for a boutique accountant in Laramie, Wyoming? You'll work with people who know your name and return your calls.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Retail

**Q: Do I need to collect sales tax in Nebraska or Colorado if my store is in Laramie?**
A: Possibly. Each state sets its own rules for when an out-of-state seller must register, and those rules change. We review where your customers are and how you sell, then confirm each state's current guidance before advising you to register or file anything.

**Q: Can you make my point of sale reports match my bank deposits?**
A: Yes. We match daily sales totals to card processor deposits, then account for fees, refunds, discounts and gift cards. When the books and the bank disagree, we find the reason and fix the cause, so the following month's reports tie out from the start.

**Q: How do I avoid a big tax bill after a strong holiday season?**
A: Check your year-to-date results before the year ends. We calculate estimated taxes and review your profit as part of our business tax work, so a large equipment or fixture purchase and a strong fourth quarter are accounted for while you still have time to act.

**Q: What should I bring to my first meeting?**
A: Bring last year's tax return plus any recent point of sale reports and sales tax returns you have. If something is missing, come anyway. The consultation is free, and we'll tell you what we'd need to get started and what to gather next.

**Q: Do you work with retailers outside Laramie?**
A: Yes. We serve retail clients across Wyoming, Nebraska and Colorado. Being based in Laramie means you deal with local people who know your community, while our three-state experience helps if you sell to customers or hold registrations in more than one state.
