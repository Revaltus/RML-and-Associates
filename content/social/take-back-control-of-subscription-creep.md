## LinkedIn

A $150,000 nonprofit budget. Eight small subscriptions. $5,664 a year, close to 4 percent of the budget, and not one board vote.

That is the illustrative example in our new post on subscription creep. The donor database at $129 a month, the design tool that renewed on a former office manager's card. Each charge is small, but together they act like an expense nobody approved.

The fix fits in one afternoon. Pull twelve months of statements, list every recurring charge, give each a named owner and renewal date, then mark it keep, downgrade, consolidate or cancel. Trimming just three lines in the example frees up roughly $1,800 a year.

There is a compliance angle too. A subscription still coded to a grant after the grant ends is the kind of thing a funder notices. Coding every software charge to "office expense" also blurs your functional allocation on the Form 990.

New treasurer or a bookkeeper who just left? Start here: https://rmlcpas.com/resources/take-back-control-of-subscription-creep
