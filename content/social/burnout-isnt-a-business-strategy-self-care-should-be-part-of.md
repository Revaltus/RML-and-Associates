## LinkedIn

A Laramie nonprofit with a $450,000 budget loses its part-time finance help in February. The executive director takes over data entry "just until we hire someone." By May 15, the Form 990 is due, nobody has filed the Form 8868 extension, and a funder is asking for year-to-date financials that do not exist yet.

Nothing in that story is about incompetence. It is one person holding five jobs.

Here is the part that surprises people: three missed annual filings in a row revokes tax-exempt status automatically. There is no warning after year two. A leader running on four hours of sleep is exactly who lets a filing slide twice and then loses track of the third.

That is why we treat rest as an internal control. A real week off forces a written handoff, a backup bank signer and a shared compliance calendar. Those are the same safeguards an auditor looks for.
