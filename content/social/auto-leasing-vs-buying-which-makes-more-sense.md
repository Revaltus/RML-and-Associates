## LinkedIn

A $42,000 cargo van for a Laramie food pantry nets out at about $22,300 to lease and about $21,500 to buy over three years. Nearly even. So why does the lease look cheaper at the dealership? The monthly payment: $549 versus about $832.

That gap is the trap. For a tax-exempt organization, Section 179, bonus depreciation and lease deductions don't lower a tax bill, because there is no tax bill. The decision comes down to cash flow, mileage, grant rules and reporting.

Change one input and the answer moves. At 20,000 miles a year, a 12,000-mile lease adds roughly $3,600 to $6,000 in overage charges over three years. If a grant ends in 24 months, leasing may be the safer call. And under ASC 842, a lease longer than 12 months shows up on your balance sheet.

If your board is weighing a van, pickup or staff sedan, our post walks through the numbers, including when mileage reimbursement beats owning anything.
