## LinkedIn

A 58-year-old Laramie program director has $450,000 in a 403(b) and a paid-off home. One three-year nursing home stay at $10,000 a month would cost $360,000. Medicare covers very little of it, since it does not pay for custodial care like bathing, dressing and eating.

Our new post compares the three sources that usually share the bill. Long-term care insurance fits healthy people in their 50s and early 60s. VA Aid and Attendance helps eligible wartime veterans and surviving spouses, with a 36-month look-back on asset transfers. Savings carry the rest.

The tax side matters too. A $120,000 year of care paid from a 403(b) is taxable income, and medical expenses only deduct above 7.5 percent of AGI.

If you lead or serve on the board of a non-profit, price local care years before the bill arrives. Read the full post: https://rmlcpas.com/resources/long-term-care-how-you-can-financially-prepare-for-the-expen
