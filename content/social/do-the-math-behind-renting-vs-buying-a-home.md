## LinkedIn

On a $300,000 home, renting came out about $18,500 cheaper over five years. That is the result of the illustrative model in our latest post, and it surprises people who assume the mortgage is the whole comparison.

The mortgage payment is the smallest part of the story. Add property tax, insurance, maintenance at 1 percent of value, and about $39,000 in down payment and closing costs that could earn 4 percent elsewhere. Then factor in 7 percent selling costs and compare the total to rent over the years you will actually stay. The gap narrows every year you stay longer, so your break-even year matters more than any market forecast.

For Laramie nonprofit staff and board members, the time horizon carries extra weight. A salary tied to a grant that ends in 18 months changes how much risk a 30-year loan carries. So does a possible move after board turnover or a new funder.

Swap in your own numbers and see where your break-even lands. Full walkthrough: https://rmlcpas.com/resources/do-the-math-behind-renting-vs-buying-a-home
