## LinkedIn

Your non-profit is sitting on 80,000 credit card points. Now what?

It happens in Laramie all the time. Someone opened a card for conference registrations and software, the rewards piled up, and nobody decided who owns them or where they belong in the books.

Here is one detail from our latest post: if a grant pays for a purchase, the rewards may not be yours to keep. Put $6,000 of program supplies on a card with 2% back, and the funder may expect the cost to net out to $5,880. Interest and late fees are typically unallowable too, so a balance carried past the due date comes out of unrestricted funds.

The fix is simple. Read the award terms first, keep grant-funded purchases on a no-rewards card or pay by check or ACH, and have the board approve a two-page card policy.
