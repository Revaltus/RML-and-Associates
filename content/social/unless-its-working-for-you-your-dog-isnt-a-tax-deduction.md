## LinkedIn

A $2,400-a-year service dog can still produce a $0 deduction. Here is the math. With an AGI of $60,000, the medical expense floor (7.5 percent) is $4,500. Add $1,800 in other medical costs and you reach $4,200, which is under the floor. It takes something like a $1,500 dental bill to cross it, and only the amount above $4,500 helps.

That is the personal side. For Laramie non-profit organizations, the question flips. Dogs show up in therapy visits, rescue programs and volunteer foster homes, and their costs belong in program service expenses on Form 990. Shifting them to management or fundraising to make overhead look lighter is the kind of thing funders and board members notice. Paid dog daycare or public obedience classes can raise Form 990-T questions, so talk it through before you launch.

Liz Powers, CPA, helps clients set up class tracking in QuickBooks Online so costs like these land in the right place the first time.
